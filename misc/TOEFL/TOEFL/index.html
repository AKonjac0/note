Why does the professor say this A. To correct a mistake in the course syllabus B. To assure the student that the class will discuss the plays he likes C. To reject the student’s objection concerning the reading assignments D. To indicate that she understands why the student is confused 您的答案： B

正确答案： C

查看解析 收起 功能题 正确答案为C，重听原文：This is a course on modern drama and that is what we are reading. 前文学生抱怨教授开设的课程不符合他的期待，他以为要读的“当代”作品实际上是严格意义上的“现代”作品。所以教授在对话结尾处再次强调：她开设的课程是当代戏剧课，我们的确在阅读必读的当代戏剧作品。她的课程描述并没有误导学生，是学生自己理解错了“当代”和“现代”的含义。对应C选项：教授驳回学生关于阅读作业的异议。

The professor explains the concept of mood congruity Which example illustrates her point? A. When people see a picture of a crying child, they feel sad B. When people feel stress, they may be reminded of a time when they missed an important deadline. C. When people feel happy, they prefer to interact with other happy people. D. A bad mood will disappear after engaging in a creative activity 您的答案： A

正确答案： B

查看解析 收起 对应原文：A phenomenon we call mood congruity一致性. Congruity means something like harmony between two things. So mood congruity works like this: if you’re feeling happy, it’s more likely that your brain will call up pleasant memories, like that wonderful beach vacation you took last year, which, of course, just reinforces your happy mood, the brain’s wired to support your mood. It’s not bound to try to balance or tempered by producing memories that aren’t consistent with the way you’re feeling at that moment. 如果你感到开心，你的大脑就更有可能唤起愉悦的回忆，比如你去年美妙的海滩度假，从而进一步加强你的开心情绪。大脑中的感受是联网的，来加强你的情绪。它不会产生跟你当下情绪相违背的回忆，来平衡或者缓和你现有的情绪。 原文讲解了mood congruity的定义，意思就是当时有什么样的情绪，你的大脑就会唤起类似的回忆，来加强你当下的情绪体验。题干问哪个例子支撑了她的观点，答案为B选项：当人们感受到压力时，他们可能会回忆起他们错过一个重要的规定期限的时刻（错过重要期限是先前人们承受压力的记忆）。 A选项：当人们看到一幅儿童哭泣的照片的时候，他们会感到悲伤。这是移情作用empathy，认同和理解别人的处境、感情和动机，不是本文讲解的mood congruity。 注意本题的考法，原文中给出术语定义+例子，选项中需要选出另一个可以论证术语的例子，这个正确选项的内容并未在原文中直接出现，需要在理解原文文意的基础上进行判断。

Why does the professor say this: A. To introduce a point she is about to make B. To warn students of a common error C. To identify an oversight in the textbook D. To propose her own theory on the subject 您的答案： A

正确答案： B

查看解析 收起 在讲座中，Stephen Jay Gould 对 Cope's Rule 提出了几项主要反对意见： 1.化石记录的不完整性：Gould 认为化石记录中存在很多间隙，这可能导致错误的解释。然而，这并不是他反对 Cope's Rule 的核心论点。 2.比较的误导性：Gould 认为 Cope 将相隔上千万年的化石进行比较是误导性的，但这也是对方法论的批评，而不是对规则本身的根本否定。 3.小动物的存在：Gould 提出一个关键观点，即 Cope's Rule 没有解释为什么地球上仍然存在许多小动物。根据物理学原理，小动物依然能够生存，因为它们的体型更适合某些环境，这直接挑战了 Cope's Rule 对体型进化的单一方向性。 4.自然灾害对大型动物的不利影响：Gould 还提出大型动物在自然灾害中更容易灭绝的观点，这与 Cope's Rule 的假设相矛盾。 综合这些反对意见，Gould 认为 Cope's Rule 过于片面，忽视了许多重要的生物学和环境因素，特别是小型动物的存在和其生存优势。因此，选项 B 是最符合 Gould 对 Cope's Rule 主要反对观点的答案。答案已核对。

Part 1: Classification by Themes (Argument Building)

Use these phrases to build the "meat" of your body paragraphs depending on the topic.

1. Education, Learning & Campus Life
  • Knowledge & Growth:
    • "Practical experience deepens understanding."
    • "Broaden their horizons" / "Expand their outlook."
    • "Shed light on complex issues."
    • "Acquire knowledge" / "Impart established knowledge."
    • "Formative years" (crucial time for student growth).
    • "All-round development."
  • Methodology:
    • "Hands-on experiences tailored to students."
    • "Learn the law of successes and failures."
    • "Enter a virtuous cycle of learning."
  • Mental State:
    • "Escape the repetitive routine of school life."
    • "Regain a vigorous mentality."
    • "Instill values/confidence."
2. Work, Business & Success
  • Competition & Strategy:
    • "Jockey for advantage" (compete intensely).
    • "Cut-throat competition."
    • "Be pitted against professionals."
    • "Stop short of" (decide not to do something extreme).
    • "Maintain the status quo."
    • "At stake" (at risk).
  • Efficiency & Operations:
    • "A multi-pronged approach to improve efficiency."
    • "Hinders the improvement of efficiency."
    • "Avoid repetition of labour."
    • "Save time and effort."
  • Career & Money:
    • "Receive promotion and career advancement."
    • "Sense of achievement."
    • "Bread and butter issues" (basic survival/money issues).
    • "Make ends meet."
    • "Prioritize compensation."
3. Environment, Urban Planning & Nature
  • Crisis & Negative Impact:
    • "Die out" / "Wipe out" (extinction).
    • "In the grip of [drought/disaster]."
    • "Wreaking havoc on people's lives."
    • "Environmental disasters."
  • Sustainability:
    • "Sustainable development."
    • "Peaceful coexistence with nature."
    • "Each species plays an important role in the food chain."
    • "Not pursue growth at the price of the environment."
    • "Silver lining" (hope in a bad situation).
  • City Life:
    • "Hustle and bustle of the city."
    • "Commuting hampers efficiency."
    • "Green spaces provide an escape from the daily grind."
4. Technology & Information
  • Progress:
    • "In the era of exponential technological growth."
    • "Advances by leaps and bounds."
    • "Keep track of [changes]."
  • Negatives:
    • "Information overload."
    • "Susceptible to exaggerated arguments."
    • "Hinders independent thinking."
    • "Expose children to negative information."
5. Family, Arts & Well-being
  • Social & Emotional:
    • "Enlarge one's social circle."
    • "Maintain intimate relationships with family."
    • "Establish a close rapport."
    • "Recharge and refresh."
    • "Alleviate stress."
  • Arts & Society:
    • "Intellectual and spiritual production."
    • "Beneficial to citizens’ mental health."
    • "Ignored for a long time."

Part 2: Classification by Function (Logical Connectors)

Use these to connect ideas. Examples range from basic to advanced/idiomatic.

1. Addition (Adding a point)
  • Basic: In addition, Moreover, Furthermore, Besides.
  • Advanced: Additionally, What's more.
  • Structural: Apart from [Point A], another reason is... / Not to mention... / To add on to my previous point...
  • Idiomatic: A laundry list of [reasons] / Plus...
  • Emphasis: Let alone... (e.g., "He can't run a mile, let alone a marathon.")
2. Contrast & Turn (Changing direction)
  • Basic: However, But, Yet.
  • Formal: Nonetheless, Nevertheless, Conversely, In contrast, On the contrary.
  • Nuanced: Albeit (Although) / Ironically / Oppositely.
  • Concession (Academic Discussion):
    • Admittedly...
    • Granted, [Name] is right regarding...
    • While I understand [Name]’s point, I still believe...
    • Still, a voice arises that...
3. Cause & Effect (Showing results)
  • Basic: So, Because, Since.
  • Formal: Therefore, Thus, Hence, Consequently, As a result, Accordingly.
  • Structural: ...which in turn leads to... / For this reason...
  • Advanced Verbs (Crucial for high scores):
    • Stem from (The problem stems from...)
    • Hinge on (Success hinges on...)
    • Be attributable to (The failure is attributable to...)
    • Account for (This accounts for the increase in...)
    • Give rise to
    • Ensue from
4. Elaboration & Examples
  • Phrases: To be specific, To be more specific, To be more precise.
  • Illustration: To illustrate, To elaborate, For instance, For example.
  • Restatement: In other words, That is to say, Put another way.
  • Structure: Take [Subject] as an example...
5. Emphasis & Scope
  • Per se (In itself).
  • By no means (Not at all).
  • Without a doubt.
  • It is widely acknowledged that...

Part 3: Integrated Writing Specifics (Reading vs. Listening)

These are strictly for the first task (Integrated Writing) to show contrast between the passage and the lecture.

  • To Challenge/Disagree:
    • The lecturer casts doubt on the reading.
    • The professor refutes the points made in the passage.
    • The speaker contradicts the author.
    • The lecturer disputes the claim that...
    • The speaker opposes the saying that...
  • To Present Arguments:
    • The author posits that... whereas the speaker argues...
    • The reading advocates for..., but the listening counters this by...
    • The lecturer identifies weaknesses in the reading's theory.
  • To Confirm (Rare, but possible):
    • The lecture bears out (confirms) the reading.

Part 4: Structural Templates (Academic Discussion)

Opening: * From my perspective... * I am inclined to support [Name]'s idea that... * I firmly hold the idea that... * As far as I am concerned...

Closing: * In conclusion, for the reasons mentioned above... * Consequently, I strongly believe...

  1. Expand one’s horizons
    作为万能理由的第一条，expand one’s horizons 绝对是不二之选。究其本意，单数的 horizon 是“地平线、海平线”的意思，但复数的 horizons 却指人们的“眼界、视野”，其英文注释为： the limit of your ideas, knowledge and experience. 略举一例：World history is a course of study that will broaden your horizons.

    先看一个具体话题：（第 10 题）Should the government help to build more museums and theaters?下面以表格的形式给出其中一条主干理由：enrich citizens’ cultural life（丰富公民的文化生活）

    Yes, I think the government should help to build more recreational facilities

    Enrich citizens’ cultural life

    • expand young people’s horizons

    • have a place to go for fun on weekends

    • art exhibitions can improve public taste

    再来看一个抽象话题：（第 3题）Describe the most important decision in your life. 面对此类抽象话题，最佳策略是将其具体化，变成一个日常生活话题，然后套用我们的万能理由。

    请看思路：I think I made the most important decision of my life by choosing to go to an overseas university after graduation from high school in China.而其中的一条主干理由就是：it will broaden my horizons.

    参见下表： Choosing to go to an overseas university after graduation from high school in China

    Broaden my horizons

    • made friends with people from different backgrounds

    • travelled a lot around Europe and North America

    • visited various museums and historical places

    作为主干理由的 broaden my horizons，之下又有 made friends, travelled a lot, visited various museums 等三条分支理由，思路极易展开。

  2. Money, finance & economy

    今天讲的第三条万能理由，开场白是： “钱不是万能的，但没有钱却是万万不能的。” 的确，用 money 的思路不可能涵盖任何话题， 但如果缺少 money 的思路，很多话题就无从展开。

    先来一道经典例题：（第 3题）The most important decision in your life.

    思路如下： Going to an overseas university

    Achieve my independence

    • started to live on my own

    • learned a lot of practical skills such as cooking

    • became financially independent through a part-time job

    如果我们选择 going to an overseas university 作为答案，其中的一条主干理由就是achieve my independence，而在所有的 independence 之中，financial independence 无疑是最直接、最现成、最有说服力的一种。简单说，就是通过在国外打工，earn money and make a living

    有些题目属于一眼就看穿思路的，如：（第 17 题）Living alone vs. sharing rooms with other people.

    Sharing rooms with other people is a better choice

    Sharing rooms is more affordable

    • rental fee is relatively high for an average student • sharing rooms can ease their financial burden • other living expenses can also be shared

    在这个回答中，我们可以学习到很多跟钱相关的表达方式：affordable, rental fee, ease financial burden,. 说穿了就是 save money, reduce costs 的意思。

  3. Relax, enjoy & have fun!

今天的万能理由之四，主要是跟“快乐”、 “幸福”、“休闲”、“放松”有关，关键词 除了标题中的 relax, enjoy, have fun 之外，还有 happy, pleasure, joy, satisfaction, recreation 等等。

中国考生比较容易忽视这个理由，可能是与我们 平时缺乏“快乐教育”有关，但在西方人眼里， 没什么东西比快乐更重要的了。因此，如果我们在口语拓展中经常运用这一“快乐”思路，就跟英文思维不谋而合啦！

我们从一道简单的题目开始： （第 5题）What do you do in your spare time?

业余时间当然是快乐时光咯，请看详细的答题思路，如下：

Well, I’d like to do various sports and recreational activities in my spare time.

I enjoy a lot of sports

• doing sports regularly is good for my health

• sports games such as basketball are fun

• I can make a lot of new friends this way

Recreation is important for me

• outdoor activities such as hiking are very relaxing

• I can have a better balance between work and leisure

• it is one of the best ways to improve my quality of life

（第 33 题）If you have a chance to choose your job freely, what kind of job will you choose? 我们选择一个 job satisfaction 比较高的职业吧：

I will choose a teaching job, probably teaching in a university

Teaching gives me a lot of satisfaction

• sharing knowledge with students is very enjoyable

• happy to see students grow academically and personally

• students often express their gratitude

该题答案娴熟运用了“快乐”思维，把做老师的满足感和成就感说得淋漓尽致。

  1. Health & wealth

今天推荐的万能理由是：health & wealth.

俗话说的好：健康是革命的本钱。 的确，用 health 的解题思路就像一个 万能胶，贴在哪个话题上总可以沾点边。 这里我们还可以把 health 细分为： physical health & mental health。英语有句谚语：Wealth is nothing without health.可以看出 health 是wealth 的根，而追求 wealth 又几乎是每个人的生活目标，所以掌握好这个关键词将使我们在口语题中事半功倍。

比如经典例题（第 19 题）Computers: improve our lives or cause a variety of problems? 分析如下：电脑的广泛运用是众所周知，但随之而来的问题是现代人长期使用电脑所引起的亚健康。在论证：Computers have caused a variety of problems.我们可以立刻套用万能理由：health 上，然后再将其陈述详细化：

Computers have caused a variety of problems

Using computer too much is bad for health

• staring at the screen for too long causes eye strains • lead to neck and back problems

• become obese and develop chronic illnesses

抓住 health 这个主干理由，详述分支理由：causes eye strains/lead to neck and back problems/develop chronic illness 使论点得到充分论证。

再看一道抽象话题：Where do you like to live: a forest, a desert or a plain? 从问电脑对生活的影响到放轻松的方式，以及现在的你希望生活在那种环境下？ 其实万变不离其宗，我们都可以将繁杂的问题归纳到 health 上，以不变应万变。

I think a forest is a much better place for living

Living among trees is a very healthy way of life

• a forest is full of fresh air

• a lot of green plants is good for my eyes

• I can do hiking every morning

从中我们可以围绕 health 依次展开，发散思维，这里有一些有用的 health 固定搭配可作拓展参考：health care（卫生保健） public health（公共卫生） good health（身体健康）human health （人类健康）。附送万能论据：We all desire wealth and health.

  1. Efficient

今天我们隆重推荐第七个万能理由： Efficient！efficient！efficient！ 演说家切斯特〃菲尔德曾说过： 效率是工作的灵魂。Efficiency is the good work of the soul – Chestfeild. 所以小站也想说有时 efficient 也是答题的诀窍。

下面让我们来看一下例题：（第 26 题）Describe one of the most important inventions in the past 100 years. 描述一下过去 100 年最重要的发明。

看似风牛马不相及的问题，其实也隐藏着巧妙的答题技巧。无论你选择阐述哪种发明，都是为了提高人类工作效率而产生。因此，这时 efficient 的这个万能理由就能充分发挥其主干理由的作用，极大扩充了答题思路。

The light bulb invented by Thomas Edison is the most important

Has greatly improved our working efficiency

• constant lighting in the workplace increases productivity

• factories can run day and night

• business hours have been extended dramatically

顺着主干理由 efficient，我们可以推论出一些很好用的搭档词组，比如：efficient production / efficient market /efficient performance 有效生产/有效市场/经营成果。供开拓思路参考。

再看一道细节题：（第 36 题）Doing school assignments: alone or in a group? 这是一道很典型的美式思维题，我们知道在美国的大学课堂里出勤率并不代表你就能得分。美式大学文化更崇尚于 join in the discussion. 常常是鼓励敢于挑战老师教学内容权威的学生。详细分析如下：

Doing school assignments with a group is much better than doing them alone

It is more efficient

• have other students to discuss issues

• can share the workload when doing a big project

• brainstorming sessions are always possible

Efficient 作为万能理由是它的实用性，下面小站为您精心挑选了一些利用率同样很高的形容词: useful /helpful/ meaningful/ valuable

  1. Trait & characteristic 今天揭晓的万能理由是：Trait & characteristic！ 心理大师弗洛伊德曾说过：Character determines destiny！（性格决定命运!） 那么就让我们来了解一下 characteristic 的万能 用法吧。

首先看一道详细描述题（第 20 题）： The characteristics of a good teacher 我们给出的完整思路如下：

There are two important qualities of being a good teacher

A good teacher must have a great amount of patience

• happy to see students growing gradually

• never lose temper in class

• answer questions and give guidance patiently

A good teacher should be able to motivate students

• students sometimes feel down

• they need encouragement and emotional support

• teachers need to motivate and inspire them in learning

本题直接提问：一名好教师的特征。那么我们就可以从 characteristic 出发，发散思维到各种优秀品质：patience/motivate 作为分支理由陈述。思路迎刃而解。

再看一道细节题（第 28 题）：Describe the characteristics of a good friend

A good friend must have two essential qualities

A good friend must be honest

• a true friend never lies to you

• they show their genuine feelings

• they should point out your mistakes directly

A good friend must be helpful

• a friend in need is a friend indeed

• be willing to help you out when you are in a crisis

• mutual support can maintain a friendship

在characteristic 的基础上，我们立刻就可拓展出 honest/helpful 等性格品质。沿着这个思路，我们收集了一些性格特质的有用形容词，使我们将来遇到任何characteristic 话题都能立刻套用：aspiring 有志气的，有抱负的 candid，正直的dedicated，有奉献精神的 expressivity 善于表达，faithful 守信的等。

  1. Interest

The last but the most important thing 今天我们推出最后的，但是最重要的 万能理由——Interest！！！Einstein said: Interest is the best teacher. 所以我们把 interest 作为最后最重要的理由推出。 Interest 顾名思义：就是兴趣的意思， 但它的作用非常强大。比如，可衍生出 motivate/create/meet challenge（动力、创造力、面对挑战）的概念。

下面我们通过经典例题来分析：（第 39 题）Choosing a subject: One that brings job opportunities or that is really interesting. Interest is the best teacher 作为主旨理由，因为 interest 可以激发一个人的学习热情，可以使他主动获取知识，而 将兴趣培养成自己的职业是一件最美好不过的事情。

I would choose a subject that really interests me

Can keep my attention and motivation

• interest is the best teacher in academic studies

• make me enthusiastic about learning

• encourage me to do in-depth research

让我们看一道特别的反面论证的题（第 45 题）：Is it necessary for universities to provide physical education?

No, it is not one of the university’s functions to provide physical education Students should develop their own interests in sports

• some basic physical education is provided by high school

• college students can choose different sports clubs freely

• it is time for adult students to make their own decisions

点评：陈述句回答是：No, it is not necessary.但这并不妨碍我们将主干理由放在Interest 上，题中给出的分支理由是：make their own decision/choose different sports clubs freely/and so on .这里我们拓展思维，这样纵向延伸思路就芝麻开门拉。

其他模板/总结

第一部分：独立口语/写作通用模板 (Independent Tasks)

1. 开篇表明观点 (Stating Your Position)

  • 万能句式 1 (清晰立场): > From my perspective, / In my view, it is more advantageous/beneficial for... to... > (在我看来，对于...来说，做...是更有利的。)

    • 例句: From my perspective, it is more beneficial for university students to choose a variety of courses rather than focusing on a single subject.

  • 万能句式 2 (权衡利弊后表态): > While some people may argue that..., I am convinced that the benefits of... far outweigh its drawbacks. > (虽然有些人可能认为...，但我坚信...的好处远大于其弊端。)

    • 例句: While some people may argue that living in a big city is stressful, I am convinced that the benefits of abundant job opportunities and cultural activities far outweigh its drawbacks.

  • 万能句式 3 (针对二选一问题): > When it comes to the question of whether A or B is better, I would not hesitate to choose A. > (当谈到是A还是B更好的问题时，我会毫不犹豫地选择A。)

    • 例句: When it comes to the question of whether to study alone or in a group, I would not hesitate to choose studying in a group.
2. 引出论点/理由 (Introducing Reasons)

  • 万能句式 1 (引出首个理由): > First and foremost, the primary reason for my stance is that... > (首先，我持此立场的主要原因是...)

    • 例句: First and foremost, the primary reason for my stance is that a diverse education cultivates critical thinking skills.

  • 万能句式 2 (引出后续理由): > Furthermore, / Moreover, / In addition, another compelling point to consider is that... > (此外，另一个值得考虑的有说服力的点是...)

    • 例句: Furthermore, another compelling point to consider is that exposure to different fields can inspire unexpected career interests.
3. 展开论述与举例 (Elaboration and Examples)

  • 万能句式 1 (通用举例): > To illustrate this point, / A case in point is that... > (为了阐明这一点，一个恰当的例子是...)

    • 例句: To illustrate this point, a history major who takes a computer science class might discover a passion for digital humanities.

  • 万能句式 2 (解释因果): > This is because... / This can be attributed to the fact that... > (这是因为... / 这可以归因于...)

    • 例句: This is because interacting with peers from different backgrounds provides a wider range of perspectives on a problem.

  • 万能句式 3 (引用个人经历 - 口语常用): > Take my own experience as an example. > (以我自己的经历为例。)

    • 例句: Take my own experience as an example. Last year, I joined a study group for my economics class, and discussing complex theories with my friends really helped me understand them better.
4. 承认对立观点/让步 (Acknowledging Counterarguments)
  • 万能句式 (先让步后反驳): > Admittedly, some might argue that... However, this argument overlooks the fact that... > (诚然，有人可能会说... 然而，这个论点忽略了...)
    • 例句: Admittedly, some might argue that early specialization allows students to gain deep expertise. However, this argument overlooks the fact that the modern job market values adaptability and interdisciplinary skills more than ever.
5. 结尾总结 (Conclusion)
  • 万能句式 (总结重申): > In conclusion, / To sum up, for the aforementioned reasons, it is my firm belief that... > (总之，基于上述原因，我坚信...)
    • 例句: In conclusion, for the aforementioned reasons of fostering critical thinking and enhancing career adaptability, it is my firm belief that a broad-based education is superior.

第二部分：综合口语/写作通用模板 (Integrated Tasks)

1. 概述阅读/听力核心观点 (Summarizing the Main Point)

  • 阅读部分: > The reading passage presents a theory that... / The author argues that... > (阅读文章提出了一个理论，即... / 作者认为...)

  • 听力部分 (反驳观点): > The lecturer, however, casts doubt on the claims made in the article. He/She argues that... > (然而，讲座中的教授对文章中的观点提出了质疑。他/她认为...)

  • 听力部分 (支持观点): > The lecturer reinforces the points made in the reading passage. He/She provides examples/evidence to support the idea that... > (讲座支持了阅读文章中的观点。他/她提供了例子/证据来证明...)

2. 连接阅读和听力的具体论点 (Connecting Specific Points)

  • 反驳模板: > First, the reading states that... In contrast, the lecturer contends that this is not accurate because... > (首先，阅读文章指出... 相反，教授认为这并不准确，因为...)

    Secondly, the author of the passage claims that... However, the professor refutes this point by saying that... (其次，文章作者声称... 然而，教授通过说明...来反驳这一点。)

  • 支持模板: > First, the reading suggests that... The lecturer builds on this idea by providing a specific example, which is... > (首先，阅读文章提出... 讲座通过提供一个具体的例子来进一步阐述这个观点，即...)

    Furthermore, the passage points out that... The professor supports this by explaining that... (此外，文章指出... 教授通过解释...来支持这一点。)

使用建议 (Pro Tips)

  1. 灵活变通 (Be Flexible): 不要一字不差地照搬。根据具体题目，替换关键词，调整语序，让它看起来像是你自己的话。
  2. 注重内容 (Content is King): 模板只是骨架，真正的高分来自于你填充进去的血肉——即有说服力的理由、具体的例子和清晰的细节。
  3. 熟能生巧 (Practice Makes Perfect): 在练习时有意识地使用这些句式，直到它们成为你语言工具箱里的一部分，可以自如地调用。

核心万能范文库 (Master Templates)

范文 1：【全面发展与多样性】(Well-roundedness & Diversity)

适用主题： 通识教育 vs. 专业教育、学习历史/文化、和不同的人相处/居住。 核心逻辑： 1. 拓宽视野 (Broaden horizons)： 接触不同领域/观点能让人思维更开阔，具备批判性思维。 2. 更有趣/软技能 (Interdisciplinary skills/Fun)： 单一领域太枯燥，跨领域知识有助于解决复杂问题，也能让人在社交中更有趣。

高分回答 (Script): "I definitely prefer the option that encourages diversity and a broader scope of knowledge. First, it really broadens our horizons. Being exposed to different subjects or people from different backgrounds helps us see the world from multiple perspectives. Second, it cultivates interdisciplinary skills. In today’s world, problems are complex and often require knowledge from various fields to solve effectively. Plus, it makes life more interesting."

范文 2：【社交与面对面交流】(Interaction & Face-to-Face)

适用主题： 线上 vs. 线下、小组合作 vs. 独处、去博物馆/图书馆是讨论还是安静。 核心逻辑： 1. 沟通效率 (Communication efficiency)： 面对面能看到肢体语言，减少误解，效率更高。 2. 情感连接/氛围 (Emotional bond/Atmosphere)： 人是社会动物，互动能带来灵感，也能加深感情。

高分回答 (Script): "I prefer interaction and face-to-face communication. The main reason is that it prevents misunderstandings. When you talk to someone in person, you can see their facial expressions and body language, which conveys a lot more information than just text or voice alone. Also, it creates a better atmosphere. Whether it’s working on a project or visiting a museum, sharing ideas and getting immediate feedback from others makes the experience more engaging and inspiring than doing it alone."

范文 3：【独立与隐私】(Independence & Privacy)

适用主题： 父母管教、查看隐私、孩子独立、寄宿学校、自己做决定。 核心逻辑： 1. 建立信任 (Builds trust)： 尊重隐私能建立健康的亲子/人际关系。 2. 培养独立性 (Cultivates independence)： 孩子需要空间去犯错并从中学习，过度保护会导致依赖。

高分回答 (Script): "I believe that independence and privacy are crucial, especially for young people. First of all, everyone needs their own personal space. Constantly monitoring someone, like reading their texts, signals a lack of trust, which can actually damage the relationship and cause resentment. Secondly, it helps cultivate self-reliance. Young people need to learn how to manage their own lives and make their own decisions. Even if they make mistakes, it’s a valuable learning opportunity that helps them grow into responsible adults."

范文 4：【兴趣与热情导向】(Passion & Interest)

适用主题： 选工作（钱 vs. 兴趣）、选课（擅长 vs. 兴趣）、志愿者 vs. 工作、文娱活动。 核心逻辑： 1. 动力与持久性 (Motivation & Longevity)： 有兴趣才能坚持，否则容易倦怠 (burn out)。 2. 心理健康 (Mental health/Satisfaction)： 快乐比金钱或单纯的成功更重要，能带来长期的满足感。

高分回答 (Script): "I would choose to prioritize passion and personal interest. To begin with, passion is the best motivator. When you genuinely enjoy what you are doing, you are more likely to put in the extra effort and persist through challenges without feeling burned out. Furthermore, it leads to greater personal satisfaction. A high salary or being 'talented' at something doesn't mean much if you are miserable every day. Doing what you love keeps you mentally healthy and happy in the long run."

范文 5：【计划与纪律】(Planning & Discipline)

适用主题： 制定计划 vs. 随性、早做 vs. 赶deadline、设定目标。 核心逻辑： 1. 减轻压力 (Reduce stress)： 提前规划可以避免最后的恐慌，应对突发状况。 2. 保证质量 (Ensure quality)： 有时间打磨细节，而不是匆忙完工。

高分回答 (Script): "I prefer planning ahead and setting clear goals. First, it significantly reduces stress. By having a schedule or starting early, I don't have to panic at the last minute. If any unexpected problems imply arise, I still have plenty of buffer time to fix them. Second, it ensures higher quality work. Rushing through tasks usually leads to careless mistakes. A structured approach allows me to review my work carefully and deliver the best result possible."

范文 6：【经验与挑战】(Experience & Challenge)

适用主题： 冒险活动、新老师 vs. 老老师、大公司 vs. 创业公司、普通家庭出领导人。 核心逻辑： 1. 成长与突破 (Growth & Breakthrough)： 走出舒适区能学到更多，锻炼抗压能力。 2. 创新 (Innovation)： 新的事物（人/公司）往往更有活力和新想法，不受传统束缚。

高分回答 (Script): "I would lean towards taking on challenges and trying new things. The primary reason is that stepping out of your comfort zone is the fastest way to grow. Whether it's a risky sport or a start-up job, you learn resilience and problem-solving skills that you can't get in a safe, stable environment. Additionally, it brings innovation. New teachers or new companies often bring fresh perspectives and energy, whereas established ones might be stuck in their old ways. It’s exciting to be part of something new."

范文 7：【健康与生活质量】(Health & Well-being)

适用主题： 科技成瘾、餐厅营养成分表、户外活动、看书 vs. 玩手机、很多关于“现代生活方式”的题目。 核心逻辑： 1. 身体/心理健康 (Physical/Mental Health)： 预防疾病（肥胖、近视），保持心理平衡。 2. 生活质量/专注力 (Quality of Life/Focus)： 避免分心，享受当下，不做科技的奴隶。

高分回答 (Script): "I prioritize options that benefit health and well-being. First, physical health is the foundation of everything. Whether it's knowing nutritional info or playing outside, we need to prevent modern issues like obesity or eye strain caused by sedentary lifestyles. Second, it improves mental focus. Being addicted to technology or shortcuts often leads to distraction and anxiety. Doing things the 'healthy way' (like reading a book) helps us slow down and truly enjoy life."

范文 8：【公平与机会】(Fairness & Opportunity)

适用主题： 谁该上大学、奖学金给谁、举报违规同事、加分作业、作弊问题。 核心逻辑： 1. 平等的机会 (Equal Opportunity)： 每个人都值得第二次机会或受教育的权利，不能因为贫穷或一次失败就被否定。 2. 社会责任/规则 (Social Responsibility/Rules)： 维护公平竞争的环境，帮助弱势群体。

高分回答 (Script): "I believe in fairness and providing equal opportunities. First, everyone deserves a chance. Whether it's a student from a poor family needing a scholarship or someone who needs a second chance at a grade, we should support people based on their needs and potential, not just their past background. Second, it maintains social justice. If we allow rule-breaking or ignore the needs of the disadvantaged, it creates a toxic environment. Upholding fairness encourages everyone to work hard."

范文 9：【便利与资源利用】(Convenience & Resources)

适用主题： 住哪里（便利店 vs. 安静）、保留电脑机房、电子书 vs. 纸质书、校园设施。 核心逻辑： 1. 效率与省时 (Efficiency/Time-saving)： 时间宝贵，便利的设施能大幅提高效率。 2. 资源可得性 (Access to Resources)： 并不是所有人都有私人资源（如高端电脑），公共设施提供了必要的支持。

高分回答 (Script): "I prefer the option that offers greater convenience and access to resources. The main reason is efficiency. Living near shops or having campus facilities means saving huge amounts of time on commuting or logistics, which can be spent on studying or resting instead. Also, it ensures access. Not everyone has high-end equipment or a car. Public facilities like computer labs or nearby supermarkets provide essential support that makes life manageable for everyone."
